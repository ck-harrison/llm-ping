How to use LLM Ping

Send an answer to another AI

  • Right-click on an AI page, choose LLM Ping, then the AI to send to. The latest answer lands in that AI's message box; press Enter to send.
  • Highlight text first to send only that part.
  • Or press Ctrl Shift 1 to 5. On a Mac it's Control, not Command, and you need to click on the page first.

Get more than "great answer!"

  • Right-click, LLM Ping, Modify, then pick critique, improve, compare or expand, then the AI.
  • Broadcast sends to every AI switched on under Broadcast targets, except the one you're on.

Let two AIs talk (Ping Pong)

  • Pick A and B, a mode and the number of rounds, then press Start.
  • Go to A's tab and send your opening message. Nothing happens until you do.
  • A bar at the bottom left of both tabs shows the round, a Stop button and Copy transcript.

Tip: keep a separate Chrome window with one tab per AI.
