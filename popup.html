How to use LLM PingIt

  • Right-click a response to PingIt entirely to another LLM
  • Highlight specific text and PingIt to another LLM
  • Choose Modify to add standard additional guidance when you PingIt
  • Choose Broadcast to PingIt to many LLMs at once
  • Choose Ping Pong to kick off a conversation between two LLMs

💡 Tip: For best results, open a dedicated browser window with a tab for each LLM you want to use. This keeps your PingIt workspace separate from your regular browsing.
